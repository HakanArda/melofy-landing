Side APlaying on your own

Ten songs. Ten seconds each.

Pick a playlist and a mode, and a round begins. Here's what happens on every question.

Listen

The clip starts. So does the clock.

A few seconds of the song play while the ring counts down from ten. The cover art stays hidden, because it would give the answer away.

Pick

Four answers. One tap.

Choose before the ring runs out. The wrong options never repeat an answer from earlier in the round, so memory can't help you rule them out.

Reveal

Right answers score. Fast ones score more.

Every right answer earns points, and the quicker you tap, the bigger the time bonus. Then the cover and the track details are revealed.

Liner notes

The details on the back of the sleeve.

read me

A1Explicit songs are off by default

Turn them on in Settings if you want them. Until then, tracks marked explicit never come up.

A2Keep the song playing

An optional setting that lets the clip play on after you answer, through the reveal.

A3Reminders you control

Notifications for challenge invites, results and your daily streak. Turn them off whenever you like.

B1Your name on the board

Choose how you appear on leaderboards, and rename once every seven days.

B2Fair by design

Answers and scores are checked on Melofy's servers, never on your phone, so the leaderboards show how people really played.

B3Your data, your call

Delete your account and all its data from Settings at any time.
